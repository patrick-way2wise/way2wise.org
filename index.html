Advancing the Human Side of Technology

Way2Wise Institute is a nonprofit research organization. We work to make sure human emotional intelligence, self-awareness and agency keep pace with AI automation.

Get involved See our work

Why now

AI is removing the rungs where people learned judgment

Agentic AI is changing professional work in software, medicine, finance and defense. Automation speeds up the technical work. But it also removes the entry-level practice where people used to build judgment, manage their emotions and learn to work as a team.

We study what that loss does to people, and we build open, practical ways to grow those skills back into everyday work.

Mission & vision

What we're here to do

Our mission

To make sure human emotional intelligence, self-awareness and psychological agency keep pace with automation. We do this through open-access research, privacy-first reflective AI and educational programs. They help people and organizations build emotional self-regulation, dignity and shared accountability at work.

Our vision

A future where AI doesn't replace human thinking or watch over workers. Instead, it serves as a trustworthy mirror for inner growth, helping build a workforce known for resilience, compassionate leadership and systems that put people first.

Our approach

Assess · Flex · Partner

Our approach draws on classic Organization Development, research on mindfulness, and privacy-first software design.

  • Assess

    We set careful, evidence-based baselines for emotional self-regulation, using validated scales and situational-judgment measures.

  • Flex

    We develop small, situation-specific skills that help people stay calm and adapt under acute stress and high-stakes decisions.

  • Partner

    We treat AI as a reflective partner that doesn't judge, not as an authority that grades people.

Privacy by design

People can't reflect honestly while they're being watched. Our research and standards aim to collect as little personal information as possible, keep each person's reflections private, and let people delete their own data. Each person's reflections are used only to personalize their own learning, never to train AI models.

Grounded action research

Based on Kurt Lewin's action research and David Kolb's experiential learning cycle, our programs treat everyday friction at work as the place where people grow. They turn real stress into reflection and concrete changes in behavior.

Whole-system thinking

We look at AI rollouts as both social and technical systems. The goal is that technology choices protect dignity, psychological safety and team cohesion.

Our work

Four pillars

The Institute for Agentic EQ & Reflective AI, our scientific advisory committee, guides our research methods, publications and ethics standards.

Academic research & open standards

  • Open psychometric benchmarks for emotional self-regulation in automated work, measured with more than self-report surveys.
  • Short reflective-AI modules built into university engineering, medical and business capstone courses.
  • Graduate and postdoctoral fellowships in agentic organization design, clinical empathy and human-computer interaction.

Workforce, healthcare & high-stakes resilience

  • Software engineering: reducing burnout and code-review friction with techniques like "summarize before you argue."
  • Healthcare: preserving clinical empathy through structured reflection on delivering bad news.
  • High-stakes operations: grounding techniques that help leaders and first responders manage acute stress.

Open models & privacy-first architecture

  • Reflective coaching built on open-weight AI models to avoid vendor lock-in and keep it accessible.
  • Knowledge bases built on peer-reviewed psychology, resilience and mindfulness research.
  • Technical standards for encryption and data pipelines that protect people's anonymity.

Public education & open resources

  • Free reflection workbooks, workshop curricula and scenario banks under Creative Commons licenses.
  • Nonpartisan research briefings for schools, funders and policymakers on ethical AI and human agency.

Governance

Independent, and open about how we work

The Institute is governed by an independent Board of Directors. Our research findings, evaluations and toolkits are published openly for public benefit.

Our commercial partner, way2wise.ai LLC, builds and runs software products. The Institute may contract with it for technical services. We keep the two separate, and that separation has rules:

  • Every agreement with way2wise.ai is reviewed and approved by directors who have no financial interest in it.
  • Terms must be at fair market value, checked against independent market data and competing bids.
  • Anyone with a conflict of interest must disclose it, and leaves the room for both discussion and vote.
  • No part of the Institute's net earnings goes to its directors, officers or any private individual.

Way2Wise Institute is preparing its application for federal tax-exempt status. Until it is approved, gifts to the Institute aren't tax-deductible.
